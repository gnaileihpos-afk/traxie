      Help & FAQ

      How do I scan trash?

      Go to Home, start your camera, take a photo of a waste item, then tap Scan Item. Read the tips, then tap Verify disposal and scan the bin you're using to earn eco points.

      How do points work?

      Scanning adds items to your Collection (+1 discovery pt). Main eco points come from verifying the correct bin — recycling, compost, landfill, and more. Rarer items award bigger bonuses when you match the right bin!

      What is the Collection?

      Every item you scan is saved to your Collection. Discover all 150 catalog items to complete your eco-dex!

      What is My World?

      Your world grows as you level up. Track your environmental impact and earn badges along the way.

      API Key

      You need an OpenAI API key in Settings to use the AI scanner. It's stored only on your device.
